Frequently Asked Questions

Some quick answers about using the demo website.

How can I book a service?

Open Book a Service, fill in the required vehicle and appointment details, then submit the form.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. These are sample prices for a frontend demonstration. A real garage must confirm its pricing.

Does this website accept online payments?

No. Online payment processing is not included in this frontend-only version.

Will the garage receive my booking?

No. The demo shows a confirmation in your browser only. A backend or booking service integration is needed to send actual requests.
